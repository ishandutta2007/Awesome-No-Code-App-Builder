# Awesome-No-Code-App-Builder

## Top No-Code App Builder Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Visual App Development, Internal Tools & Self-Hosted Low-Code Platforms*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial no-code and low-code app builders** and **open-source projects** that let teams build web and mobile applications, internal tools, and business apps without writing code — using drag-and-drop interfaces, data connections, and pre-built components.



**Examples** include Amazon Honeycode, Airtable, AppSheet (Google Cloud), Microsoft Power Apps, Glide, Bubble, Noloco, Softr, Quickbase, and Caspio (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: No-code and low-code app building is one of the strongest open-source domains. **Appsmith**, **Budibase**, **ToolJet**, and **Refine** lead as production-grade internal tool builders. **NocoDB**, **Baserow**, and **Teable** deliver Airtable alternatives. **Directus** and **Payload** handle headless CMS and data platforms. **Saltcorn**, **Corteza**, and **ILLA** provide additional options. **Yeoman** and **VTJ.PRO** handle AI-powered code generation. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Amazon Honeycode](https://www.honeycode.aws/)**  

  **AWS's no-code app builder** — build web and mobile apps with spreadsheet-like interface . **Note**: Honeycode is no longer accepting new customers . **Historically significant** .



- **[Airtable](https://airtable.com/)**  

  **The leading no-code database** — relational linking, views, and automations . **Best for flexible data apps** .



- **[AppSheet (Google Cloud)](https://www.appsheet.com/)**  

  **Google's no-code app platform** — build apps from spreadsheets and databases . **Best for Google Workspace users** .



- **[Microsoft Power Apps](https://powerapps.microsoft.com/)**  

  **Microsoft's low-code app platform** — canvas and model-driven apps with Power Platform integration . **Best for Microsoft ecosystem users** .



- **[Glide](https://www.glideapps.com/)**  

  **No-code app builder** — build apps from Google Sheets and databases with beautiful UI . **Best for simple mobile apps** .



- **[Bubble](https://bubble.io/)**  

  **Full-stack no-code app builder** — web apps with database, logic, and workflows . **Best for complex web apps** .



- **[Noloco](https://noloco.io/)**  

  **No-code app builder for internal tools** — build from Airtable, PostgreSQL, and other data sources . **Best for internal tools** .



- **[Softr](https://www.softr.io/)**  

  **No-code app builder for Airtable and Google Sheets** — client portals and internal tools . **Best for portals and directories** .



- **[Quickbase](https://www.quickbase.com/)**  

  **No-code platform for business apps** — enterprise-grade with governance . **Best for enterprise no-code** .



- **[Caspio](https://www.caspio.com/)**  

  **No-code database app builder** — web apps with database backend . **Best for database-driven apps** .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



### Internal Tool Builders



- **[Appsmith](https://github.com/appsmithorg/appsmith)**  

  **The leading open-source internal tool builder**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **35,000+ GitHub stars** . **Drag-and-drop UI builder with 45+ widgets** . **Connects to any database or API** (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, REST, GraphQL) . **JavaScript for custom logic** . **Self-hosted or cloud** . **AI features for building faster** . **The de facto open-source Retool alternative** . **Best for internal tools and admin panels** .



- **[Budibase](https://github.com/Budibase/budibase)**  

  **Open-source low-code platform**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **25,000+ GitHub stars** . **Build internal tools, business apps, and admin panels** . **Connects to PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, REST APIs, and more** . **Automations, RBAC, and self-hosting** . **Best for internal business apps** .



- **[ToolJet](https://github.com/ToolJet/ToolJet)**  

  **Open-source low-code platform for internal tools**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **35,000+ GitHub stars** . **Drag-and-drop builder with 60+ UI components** . **Connects to databases, APIs, and SaaS tools** . **Self-hosted or cloud** . **Best for internal tools** .



- **[Refine](https://github.com/refinedev/refine)**  

  **React-based framework for building admin panels**, MIT licensed with **30,000+ GitHub stars** . **Fully customizable with React** . **Connects to 15+ backend services** . **Best for developer-built admin panels** .



- **[ILLA Cloud](https://github.com/illacloud/illa-builder)**  

  **Open-source low-code platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **12,000+ GitHub stars** . **Build internal tools with drag-and-drop** . **Best for internal tools** .



- **[Openkoda](https://github.com/openkoda/openkoda)**  

  **Open-source low-code application platform**, MIT licensed . **Build business applications with Java and Spring Boot** . **Best for enterprise low-code** .



### Airtable Alternatives



- **[NocoDB](https://github.com/nocodb/nocodb)**  

  **Open-source Airtable alternative**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **50,000+ GitHub stars** . **Turns any SQL database into a smart spreadsheet** . **Connects to PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, SQL Server, and more** . **Grid, gallery, kanban, and form views** . **APIs and webhooks** . **The most popular open-source Airtable replacement** . **Best for database-driven apps** .



- **[Baserow](https://github.com/bramw/baserow)**  

  **Open-source no-code database**, MIT/AGPL licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **Airtable-like interface with self-hosting** . **Best for structured data apps** .



- **[Teable](https://github.com/teableio/teable)**  

  **Open-source Airtable alternative**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **PostgreSQL-based with real-time collaboration** . **Best for database apps** .



- **[Grist](https://github.com/gristlabs/grist-core)**  

  **Open-source spreadsheet-database hybrid**, Apache-2.0 licensed with **7,000+ GitHub stars** . **Combines spreadsheet flexibility with database structure** . **Best for data apps** .



### Headless CMS & Data Platforms



- **[Directus](https://github.com/directus/directus)**  

  **Open-source headless CMS and data platform**, BSL licensed with **30,000+ GitHub stars** . **Instant APIs for any SQL database** . **Admin app for managing content and data** . **Best for headless CMS** .



- **[Payload](https://github.com/payloadcms/payload)**  

  **Open-source headless CMS**, MIT licensed with **25,000+ GitHub stars** . **TypeScript-based with code-first approach** . **Best for developer-friendly CMS** .



- **[Strapi](https://github.com/strapi/strapi)**  

  **Open-source headless CMS**, MIT licensed with **65,000+ GitHub Stars** . **Customizable APIs and admin panel** . **Best for headless CMS** .

**Frameworks for building custom no-code app builder solutions**: Combine **Appsmith** or **Budibase** for internal tool building with drag-and-drop UI . Use **NocoDB** or **Baserow** for Airtable-like database apps . Deploy **ToolJet** for low-code internal tools . Choose **Directus** or **Payload** for headless CMS . Integrate **Refine** for React-based admin panels . Note that true enterprise no-code platforms with governance, compliance, and vendor-supported SLAs (Power Apps, Quickbase, AppSheet) remain primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong visual builders, data connectors, and self-hosting foundations that require integration for complete app development.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- No-code platforms handle business data and may process sensitive information. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations.

- **License considerations**: Appsmith uses Apache-2.0, Budibase uses GPL-3.0, ToolJet uses AGPL-3.0, NocoDB uses AGPL-3.0, and Directus uses BSL. Verify licensing against your use case before committing .

- **No-code platforms have trade-offs** — reduced flexibility, vendor lock-in risk, and scalability limits for complex applications. Evaluate whether low-code or custom development better fits your needs .

- **Data ownership matters** — self-hosted solutions keep data on your infrastructure; cloud platforms may store data on vendor servers. Review data processing agreements .

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong visual builders, data connectors, and self-hosting foundations, but **enterprise governance, compliance certifications, and vendor-supported SLAs** remain primarily commercial offerings.
